Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

ScrollFree keeps your data on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your settings, filters and usage history.
  • Your Instagram sign-in. It goes straight to Instagram; we never see or store it.
  • Camera and motion data for the squats unlock. It is processed on the device and never saved or sent.

What leaves your iPhone

  • iCloud backup: if iCloud is on, your ScrollFree settings are backed up to your own iCloud account. We can't read it.
  • Feedback: when you send feedback from Settings › Help, we receive what you write and, if you leave them on, the details shown on that screen (app version, iOS version, device type). It is stored by our host, Netlify, and isn't linked to your identity.
  • Filter updates: ScrollFree downloads a small settings file from our website. Nothing about you is sent.

Instagram

Instagram is Meta's website, opened inside ScrollFree. Meta's privacy policy applies to what you do there.

Tracking and children

ScrollFree doesn't track you across apps or websites and doesn't sell data. It isn't directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings › Privacy and Security › Erase deletes everything ScrollFree keeps on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it too. To remove feedback you sent, email us.
