Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Tally is built so your data stays on your iPhone. We don't run accounts, ads or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your settings, limits, schedules and usage history.
  • Your sign-ins to the social apps you open in Tally. They go straight to those services; we never see or store them.
  • Camera and motion data for unlock challenges (squats, walking). They are processed on the device and never saved or sent.
  • Screen Time data, which Apple keeps on the device and Tally can't send anywhere.

What leaves your iPhone

  • iCloud backup: if you have iCloud on, your Tally settings are backed up to your own iCloud account. We can't read it.
  • Purchases: subscriptions go through Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have Premium, using an anonymous ID.
  • Reports: when you send feedback or a bug report from Settings › Help, we receive the message you write, the app version and device model. It isn't linked to your identity.
  • App settings: Tally downloads small settings files (such as filter updates) from our server. Nothing about you is sent.

Social apps

The apps you open in Tally (such as Instagram or YouTube) are their companies' websites. Their own privacy policies apply to what you do there.

Tracking and children

Tally doesn't track you across apps or websites and doesn't sell data. Tally isn't directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings › Privacy and Security › Erase deletes everything Tally keeps on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it too. To remove a report you sent, email us.
