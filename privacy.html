Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

ScrollFree is built so your data stays on your iPhone. We don't run accounts, ads or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your settings, limits, schedules and usage history.
  • Your sign-ins to the social apps you open in ScrollFree. They go straight to those services; we never see or store them.
  • Camera and motion data for unlock challenges (squats, walking). They are processed on the device and never saved or sent.
  • Screen Time data, which Apple keeps on the device and ScrollFree can't send anywhere.

What leaves your iPhone

  • iCloud backup: if you have iCloud on, your ScrollFree settings are backed up to your own iCloud account. We can't read it.
  • Purchases: handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details or purchase history.
  • Reports: when you send feedback or a bug report from Settings › Help, we receive only what you write and, if you leave them on, the details shown on that screen (app version, iOS version, device type). It isn't linked to your identity.
  • App settings: ScrollFree downloads small settings files (such as filter updates) from our server. Nothing about you is sent.

Social apps

The apps you open in ScrollFree (such as Instagram or YouTube) are their companies' websites. Their own privacy policies apply to what you do there.

Tracking and children

ScrollFree doesn't track you across apps or websites and doesn't sell data. ScrollFree isn't directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Settings › Privacy and Security › Erase deletes everything ScrollFree keeps on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes it too. To remove a report you sent, email us.
