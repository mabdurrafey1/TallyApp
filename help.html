Help

A filter stopped working

Instagram changes its pages often. Send feedback from Settings › Help and we'll update the filter, usually without an app update.

Signing in

You sign in to Instagram itself inside ScrollFree. If it keeps asking, sign out in Settings › Apps and sign in again.

Moving to a new iPhone

With iCloud on, your settings come back when you install ScrollFree on the new phone.
